# The five client emails

These are the base versions. Rewrite each in the photographer's voice before using it; keep the structure, the facts and the length.

Placeholders: [Client first name], [Family name], [Session date], [Session time], [Location], [Questionnaire link], [Plan link], [Order-by date], [Gallery date], [Your name], [Business name].

A "Button:" line is the main link. In a plain email, write it as the button words followed by the link on its own line.

---

## Email 1: Six quick questions

**When:** right after booking, or 4 to 6 weeks before the session.

**Subject:** Let's figure out what everyone's wearing
**Preview text:** Six quick questions and I'll take it from there.

Hi [Client first name],

I'm so excited for your session on [Session date]! Before we get there, let's tackle the question every family asks: what should we wear?

You don't have to figure it out. Answer six quick questions about your crew, your style and where we're shooting, and I'll send back an outfit plan for the whole family. Every piece is ready to shop from your phone.

Button: ANSWER THE SIX QUESTIONS → [Questionnaire link]

It takes about five minutes. If you already have a favorite piece you'd love to wear, tell me about it there and I'll build the plan around it.

Talk soon,
[Your name]
[Business name]

**Text:** Hi [Client first name]! Let's figure out outfits for [Session date]. Six quick questions and I'll send back a plan for the whole family: [Questionnaire link]

**Rewrite notes**
- Keep the "six questions, five minutes" promise. It's what gets the form filled.
- Keep the favorite-piece line. It tells clients they don't have to buy everything new.
- [Questionnaire link] is her questionnaire page's public link. If she doesn't have one, the Just Wear This plugin can make it ("make my client questionnaire").

## Email 2: Your outfit plan is ready

**When:** as soon as the plan is made, ideally within a day or two of the questionnaire.

**Subject:** Your outfit plan is here
**Preview text:** Tap any piece to shop it.

Hi [Client first name],

It's ready! Here's what everyone's wearing for [Location] on [Session date]:

Button: SEE YOUR OUTFIT PLAN → [Plan link]

A few things to know:

- **Tap any piece to shop it.** Each one has an everyday, a mid and a splurge option, so you can mix and match to fit your budget.
- **Check things off as you go.** The plan keeps track of what you have, what's ordered and what's still to find.
- **Already own something close?** Use it. The colors and the mix matter more than the exact piece.

I'd aim to have everything ordered by [Order-by date] so there's time for returns and swaps.

Any questions, or something not quite right? Just reply and I'll tweak it.

[Your name]

**Text:** Your outfit plan is ready! Tap any piece to shop it: [Plan link]. Aim to order by [Order-by date] so there's time for swaps.

**Rewrite notes**
- The order-by date is the one hard detail: session date minus her lead time (default 3 weeks).
- Keep the "already own something close?" line. It lowers the pressure and the cost.
- If a piece in the plan has fewer than three price options, say "a few price options" instead of naming all three.

## Email 3: One week to go

**When:** 7 days before the session.

**Subject:** One week! Here's your outfit checklist
**Preview text:** A quick try-on now saves a scramble later.

Hi [Client first name],

One week until your session! Here's a quick checklist so the day feels easy:

- **Do a try-on this weekend.** Lay everyone's outfit out together, or snap a photo. If something feels off, there's still time to swap it.
- **Wash, steam and hang.** Wrinkles show up in photos more than you'd think.
- **Shoes count.** Make sure they fit, they're clean and little ones can walk in them.
- **Pack a comfort bag.** Snacks, wipes, a hair tie, and a jacket if it's chilly.

Your plan is right here if you need it: [Plan link]

We're meeting at [Location] at [Session time]. I can't wait!

[Your name]

**Text:** One week to go! Try everything on together this weekend so there's time to swap anything. Your plan: [Plan link]

**Rewrite notes**
- If she sends her own prep email, fold her usual details in here (parking, weather backup) so this replaces an email instead of adding one.
- Keep the checklist to four items. More gets skimmed.

## Email 4: Still looking for anything?

**When:** about 2 weeks before the session, only if the family hasn't finished shopping. Skip it for families who are done.

**Subject:** Still hunting for a piece or two?
**Preview text:** I can swap anything that's sold out or not working.

Hi [Client first name],

Just checking in! If a piece in your plan sold out, didn't fit or just isn't feeling right, tell me which one and I'll send a few swaps that still work with everyone else.

Button: OPEN YOUR PLAN → [Plan link]

No stress if you're still deciding. Ordering in the next few days keeps you clear of shipping delays.

[Your name]

**Text:** Still hunting for a piece or two? Tell me which one and I'll send swaps that work with everyone else.

**Rewrite notes**
- This is the email that saves the most session-week panic. Keep it light and helpful, never a scold.
- "Didn't fit" is about the clothes. Keep it about clothes, never bodies.
- When the client replies with a piece, the photographer can ask the Just Wear This plugin for the swap and republish the same plan link.

---

## Email 5: Thank you (and a peek)

**When:** 1 to 2 days after the session, before the gallery is ready.

**Subject:** Thank you for yesterday!
**Preview text:** A sneak peek, and when your gallery is coming.

Hi [Client first name],

Thank you for such a fun session at [Location]! Everyone's outfits came together beautifully. The colors looked so good against [one detail from the session], and I can't wait for you to see them.

Here's a little sneak peek: [Sneak peek link or attached photo]

Your full gallery will be ready by [Gallery date]. I'll send it the minute it's done.

Thank you for trusting me with your family.

[Your name]

**Text:** Thank you for such a fun session! Everyone's outfits came together so well. Sneak peek coming soon, full gallery by [Gallery date].

**Rewrite notes**
- Compliment the outfits and the colors, never how anyone looks in them.
- Use one real detail from the session (the light, the spot, a funny moment) so it never reads like a template. Ask for it if you don't have it.
- Change "yesterday" in the subject to match when she sends it.
- The gallery date sets expectations and cuts down "is it ready yet?" emails.
